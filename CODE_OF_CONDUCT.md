# Code of Conduct

> 🌐 Language / Ngôn ngữ: **English** | [Tiếng Việt](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.vi.md)

## Expected behavior

Project discussions should remain technical, respectful, and focused on reproducible evidence. Critique code, documentation, benchmarks, and claims without attacking contributors.

Helpful participation includes:

- reporting reproducible problems with enough environment detail to investigate;
- distinguishing measured facts from hypotheses;
- acknowledging uncertainty and failed experiments;
- preserving rejected evidence instead of rewriting history to look cleaner;
- respecting licensing, attribution, privacy, and security boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate publication of private information, credential sharing, malicious disruption, and knowingly falsified benchmark or acceptance evidence are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, close, or remove contributions that violate this policy and may restrict participation when necessary. For sensitive conduct concerns, contact `i.am@dangkhoa.dev` privately.
